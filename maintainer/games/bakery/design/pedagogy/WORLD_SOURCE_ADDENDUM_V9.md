## Owner-authorized mechanics recorded in the guide

- Holding **A** charges a throw and releasing it sends the carried item along the gold-ring aim arc; a quick tap also throws. The worker carries copied numbers overhead.
- The number dispenser is the visually numbered stand at the mixer’s right. Delivery remains neutral. Walking to an unfinished order and pressing **B** runs the left value, comparison, and right value in order, then briefly reports **TRUE** or **FALSE** with a quiet checkmark or cross. **B** checks an unfinished order; it does not clear it.
- Finishing the first three checks and pressing **A** unlocks four side ingredient stations. Each station icon represents the value on its side counter. Station operators switch every 12 seconds; throwing a copied number at a station changes that counter through its allowed operator. A later order can reference a random station icon, and its left side can display `3 + [icon]`, meaning three plus that station’s value. Whole-number division remains the rule.
- Supplied factory variables remain distinct from the learner’s ``||variables(noclick):batch||`` variable. The supplied code and existing native events/art are unchanged.
